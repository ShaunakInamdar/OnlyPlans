## 🚀 About Only Plans

Only Plans is a personal goal achievement app designed for people with ADHD — and anyone who struggles to stay focused, prioritize tasks, and follow through on their goals.

**Core pillars:**
- 🧠 Understands you personally through onboarding
- ✅ Breaks big goals into small, actionable steps
- 📞 Proactive AI accountability calls throughout your day
- 🏆 Reward system scaled to task difficulty
- 📅 Calendar integration for real-life scheduling
- 🗂️ Clear overview — always know what matters most right now
